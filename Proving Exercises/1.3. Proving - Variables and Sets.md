**∗1. Analyze the logical forms of the following statements: 
(a) 3 is a common divisor of 6, 9, and 15. 
(b) x is divisible by both 2 and 3 but not 4. 
(c) x and y are natural numbers, and exactly one of them is prime.**

(a) Let predicate P(x,y) stand for x as a divisor of y.
Thus, (P(3, 6) AND P(3, 9) AND P(3, 15))

(b) Let predicate D(x, y) stand  for x divisible by y.
Thus (D(x, 2) AND D(x, 3) AND NOT D(x, 4))

(c) Let predicate P(x) stand for x being prime.
Thus (x ∈ ℕ AND y ∈ ℕ) AND (P(x) XOR P(y))

**2. Analyze the logical forms of the following statements: 
(a) x and y are men, and either x is taller than y or y is taller than x. 
(b) Either x or y has brown eyes, and either x or y has red hair. 
(c) Either x or y has both brown eyes and red hair.**

(a) Let M be the set of all men, and T(x, y): x is taller than y.
Thus, (x ∈ M AND y ∈ M) AND (T(x, y) OR T(y, x)).

(b) Let B be the set of all brown eyed people, and R be the set of all red-haired people
Thus, (x ∈ B OR y ∈ B) AND (x ∈ R OR y ∈ R).

(c) Let B be the set of all brown eyed people, and R be the set of all red-haired people.
Thus, (x ∈ B AND x ∈ R) OR (y ∈ B AND y ∈ R).
