## Learnings/Things to Consider
1. "Either A or B" in english -> A XOR B (not just OR)
2. "Neither A nor B" in english -> NOT A AND NOT B

**1.Analyze the logical forms of the following statements:** 
**(a) We'll have either a reading assignment or homework problems, but we won't have both homework problems and a test.** 
**(b) You won't go skiing, or you will and there won't be any snow.
(c) sqrt(7) <= 2**

(a) Let propositions R, H, T respectively stand for having a reading assignment, homework problems, and test.
Thus, (R XOR H) AND NOT(H AND T).

(b) Let propositions A, B respectively stand for me skiing and having snow
Thus, (NOT A) OR (A AND NOT B).

(c) (sqrt(7) < 2) OR (sqrt(7) = 2).

**2. Analyze the logical forms of the following statements: 
(a) Either John and Bill are both telling the truth, or neither of them is. 
(b) I’ll have either fish or chicken, but I won’t have both fish and mashed potatoes. 
(c) 3 is a common divisor of 6, 9, and 15.**

(a) Let propositions J, B respectively stand for John telling the truth and Bill telling the truth.
Thus, (J AND B) XOR (NOT J AND NOT B)

(b) Let propositions F, C, M stand for having fish, chicken, and mashed potatoes
Thus (F XOR C) AND NOT(F AND M).

(c) Let propositions A, B, C respectively stand for 3 is a divisor of 6, 3 is a divisor of 9, and 3 is a divisor of 15.
Thus, (A AND B AND C)

(c) Either Alice or Bob is not in the room. 
(d) Neither Alice nor Bob is in the room**

(b) (NOT A AND NOT B)
(c) (NOT A XOR NOT B)
(d) (NOT A AND NOT B)

**4. Which of the following expressions are well-formed formulas? 
(a) ¬(¬P ∨ ¬¬R). 
(b) ¬(P, Q, ∧R). 
(c) P ∧¬P. 
(d) (P ∧ Q)(P ∨ R)**

(a) Yes
(b) No. Commas are not legal
(c) Yes
(d) No. every pair of proposition or propositional phrase must be operated by a logic operator (not just empty space).
